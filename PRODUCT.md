# nextjs-vps-project

## Register

brand

## Purpose

A deployment verification surface for a self-hosted Next.js app on Oracle Cloud VPS. Confirms the full stack (Nginx, PM2, Certbot, Cloudflare, DigitalPlat) is live and that SSR/hydration works in production.

## Target users

Engineers and operators validating a VPS deployment after release.

## Brand personality

Cold, precise, operational. A datacenter aisle at night: console glow, verified uptime, no marketing fluff.

## Anti-references

SaaS hero metrics, cream landing pages, eyebrow labels on every section, identical icon-card grids.

## Strategic principles

- Dark mode only for this surface
- Status and timestamps must be immediately scannable
- Infrastructure names are the content; decoration stays minimal
